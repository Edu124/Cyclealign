## Description

```
CycleAlign is decision intelligence for women who lead — mapping the four
phases of your menstrual cycle onto your actual working life, so you know
when to pitch, when to plan, when to push, and when to rest.

WORK WITH YOUR CYCLE, NOT AGAINST IT
Get a live phase reading — Menstrual, Follicular, Ovulatory, or Luteal —
computed from your own cycle, right on your device.

TASK SYNC — TIME YOUR BIGGEST MOMENTS
Pick a category (a big decision, a high-stakes conversation, deep focus
work) and CycleAlign scores your chosen date against your predicted phase
— Great timing, Manageable, or Tough window — and suggests better dates
when it matters.

DAILY CHECK-INS THAT LEARN YOU
A 20-second Quick Log (energy, mood, one win) builds a picture of your
real pattern over time — not just the textbook average.

CALENDAR AWARE
Connect Google or Apple Calendar (read-only) to see your existing events
scored against your cycle — plan around what's already on your plate.

ASK ALIGN — YOUR PRIVATE CYCLE COACH
A warm, judgment-free AI coach that knows your current phase and recent
check-ins, answering your questions about energy, mood, and planning.

A LITTLE JOY, TOO
Retail Therapy surprises you with a feel-good (zero real charge) flash
sale on a rough day — because gentleness matters as much as planning.

BUILT ON PRIVACY
Your cycle predictions are computed on your device. Full data rights —
access, correction, deletion, export — are always one tap away.

For the women, by the women.
```

## App Privacy ("Nutrition Label")

### Data Used to Track You
**None.** CycleAlign does not use data for cross-app/cross-site tracking or third-party advertising.

### Data Linked to Your Identity
| Data type | Collected? | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Contact Info — Name, Email | Yes | Account creation, sign-in |
| Health & Fitness — Cycle/reproductive health data | Yes | Core app functionality (predictions) |
| User Content — Notes/journal entries, Customer Support | Yes (Quick Log notes, AI coach chats) | App functionality |
| Identifiers — User ID | Yes | Account functionality |
| Usage Data | No | — |
| Diagnostics | No | — |

### Data Not Linked to Your Identity
None currently collected.

### Third-party data sharing
- **Anthropic (Claude API):** AI Coach questions + cycle context, only when the user actively uses "Ask Align". Not used to train third-party models per our processing agreement.
- **Google / Apple:** authentication only, when the user chooses those sign-in options.
- No data is sold. No data is shared for advertising.
