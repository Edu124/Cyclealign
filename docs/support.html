Frequently asked

How is my cycle data used?
Your cycle predictions are computed on your own device. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what's stored and why.

Can I delete my account?
Yes — open the app, go to Profile → Privacy Settings → Delete all my data. This is permanent and immediate.

Is CycleAlign free?
The core app is free. Some features are part of an optional premium plan, accessible from the Profile tab.
