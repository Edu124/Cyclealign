CycleAlign

Work with your cycle. Not against it.

CycleAlign is a cycle-awareness and planning app for iOS and Android. It predicts where you are in your menstrual cycle and turns that into practical, day-to-day guidance — when to plan demanding work, when to expect lower energy, and when your body could use a lighter day.

What CycleAlign does

Phase-aware planning. CycleAlign predicts your current cycle phase from the dates you log and surfaces what tends to help or hurt on each phase — deep-focus work, high-stakes conversations, or rest.

Daily check-ins. A quick log of energy, mood, and notes builds a personal history you can look back on, and sharpens future predictions.

Calendar-aware task planning. If you choose to connect your Google or Apple Calendar, CycleAlign reads your upcoming events (read-only) and scores them against your predicted phase, so you can see at a glance which days on your calendar might be harder or easier — without moving or editing anything on your calendar.

Ask Align. An in-app AI coach for cycle-related questions, grounded in your own logged data.

Community & resources. A moderated space to talk with others, a founder blog, and curated wellness recommendations by phase.

Why we ask for the data we ask for

CycleAlign only requests the data each feature actually needs, and most of it never leaves your device:

  • Cycle dates and daily check-ins power your phase predictions and are what makes the app useful — used only to generate your own guidance, never sold or used for advertising.
  • Calendar access is entirely optional, read-only, and used solely to line your existing events up against your predicted phase inside the app.
  • Google/Apple sign-in is used only for authentication.

Full detail on what's collected, how it's stored, and your rights over it is in our Privacy Policy.

Not a medical device

CycleAlign offers general wellness and productivity guidance based on hormonal cycle patterns. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a form of contraception. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns, symptoms, or reproductive health questions.
